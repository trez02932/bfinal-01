FAQ

Everything you might want to know about our General Knowledge Quiz.

Is this quiz suitable for everyone?

Absolutely! We focus on creating a broad, educational, and family-friendly environment for everyone to learn and explore.

Does it work on my tablet?

Yes! Our quiz is designed to work perfectly on tablets, phones, and computers using any modern browser.

Do I need to download anything?

Nope! The quiz plays right in your web browser. No installs or app store visits needed.

Are there any hidden costs?

Our quiz is free to play! We want to make sure everyone can enjoy the fun without worry.

How are high scores saved?

Your scores are saved right on your own device, so you can see your progress next time you visit.

Can I contact you?

Yes! We welcome feedback from our users. You can reach out through our contact page or email us directly.

How do I answer questions?

Simply click or tap on the answer you think is correct. It's designed to be easy and intuitive on any device!

Can I play with my friends?

While the current quiz is single-player, you can challenge your friends to beat your high score!

Does the quiz help me learn?

Yes! Our quiz is designed to test and expand your general knowledge on a wide range of topics in a fun way.

How often are new quizzes added?

We are always working on new ideas and hope to add more fun quizzes and topics frequently!

Is my data private?

We take privacy seriously. We don't collect personal information from our players. Please see our Privacy Policy for more details.

What if I find a bug?

If something isn't working right, please let us know via the contact form so we can fix it for everyone!
